AJM FIT - PHOTOS FOR THE TWELVE NEW MEALS
=========================================

Two sheets of six. Six per sheet is what produced the sharpest cells so
far: forty-four in one image gave 139px each and was unusable, twenty-five
gave 258px and was soft, six gave 512px and was good. Ask for the largest
output the tool will give.

Send each sheet back and it gets cut, checked against the meal it claims
to be, and uploaded.


============================================================
SHEET A  -  the five quick dinners, plus one snack
============================================================

A single image containing 6 food photographs arranged as a 3 by 2 grid of
equal square cells, no gaps, no labels, no text. Each cell is an overhead
photo on a plain matte dark grey surface with soft even daylight from the
left, shallow depth of field, no hands, no cutlery, no napkins,
photorealistic. The six dishes, reading left to right then top to bottom:
1) tinned salmon with rice and peas in a bowl, 2) sliced cooked chicken
breast with a green salad and wholemeal pitta, 3) scrambled eggs with
baked beans and wholemeal toast, 4) tofu and mixed stir fry vegetables in
a bowl, 5) grilled halloumi with roasted vegetables and couscous,
6) strips of cooked chicken breast with a pot of red salsa.
Make the output as large and high resolution as possible.

  cell 1 -> d10.jpg   Tinned salmon with microwave rice and peas
  cell 2 -> d11.jpg   Shop-cooked chicken with bagged salad and pitta
  cell 3 -> d12.jpg   Eggs, beans and toast
  cell 4 -> d13.jpg   Frozen stir fry vegetables with tofu
  cell 5 -> d14.jpg   Halloumi and roasted vegetable couscous
  cell 6 -> s10.jpg   Chicken strips with salsa
