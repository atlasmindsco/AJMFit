# Drip 047 — Closed-loop motor control
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 128. Theme: Programming & Variables. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Master your movements with feedback

---

Every time you perform a movement, your brain is actively learning how to do it better. **Closed-loop motor control** is a process where sensory feedback helps you refine your motor programs, allowing your nervous system to execute movements automatically. This means that the more accurately you practice a movement, the better your body learns to perform it without thinking.

If you practice a movement incorrectly, your brain will also learn those mistakes. This is why focusing on perfect practice is key to developing optimal movement patterns. Think of it as programming your brain with the right instructions.

**Do this week:** Choose one exercise you want to improve, and perform it with full attention to form and execution. Use a mirror or video to provide feedback on your technique.
