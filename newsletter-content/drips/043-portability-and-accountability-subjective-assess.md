# Drip 043 — Portability and Accountability SUBJECTIVE ASSESSMENTS
Source: High Res_CPT, chunk 258. Theme: Assessment & Safety. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Why your assessment matters

---

Imagine stepping into a gym where every detail about your fitness journey is tracked and respected. **Subjective assessments** rely on personal observations and opinions to gauge your fitness level. This means your trainer will ask questions and consider your feelings about your workouts, not just the numbers on a scale.

For your training, this adds a human touch. It helps your trainer understand your experiences, preferences, and challenges, leading to a more tailored program. Your insights can reveal what really motivates you or what holds you back, making your path to fitness more effective.

**Do this week:** Share your thoughts with your trainer about your current program—what you enjoy, struggle with, and any goals you have.

Next week, we'll dive into objective assessments and how they complement your training.
