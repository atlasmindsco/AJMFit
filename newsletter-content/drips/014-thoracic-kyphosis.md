# Drip 014 — Thoracic kyphosis
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Anatomy & Movement. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Fixing Your Posture Starts Here

---

Slouching over your phone can cause serious issues. **Thoracic kyphosis** is an abnormal forward curvature of the thoracic spine, which can lead to neck, spine, and shoulder problems. This posture puts unnecessary stress on your body and can decrease your overall movement quality.

Improving your thoracic spine posture can enhance your performance and reduce the risk of injury. Maintaining a neutral spine during workouts and daily activities is key to staying healthy and functional.

**Do this week:** Spend five minutes daily doing wall slides to open up your chest and strengthen your upper back.
