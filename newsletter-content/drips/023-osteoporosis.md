# Drip 023 — Osteoporosis
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 27. Theme: Programming & Variables. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Build stronger bones today

---

Imagine your bones as living structures that need the right nutrients to stay strong. **Osteoporosis** is a bone disease characterized by a loss in bone mass and density, making your bones weaker and more prone to fractures. This happens when your diet lacks key nutrients like protein, calcium, and vitamin D. If you want to keep your bones healthy, focus on what you eat.

**Do this week:** Incorporate a source of calcium and vitamin D into your meals, like leafy greens or fortified dairy products.
