# Drip 046 — Synapse
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 16. Theme: Anatomy & Movement. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Train your brain and muscles

---

Every time you move, signals fire between your brain and muscles. This communication happens at a **synapse**, which is the connection point between two neurons or between a neuron and a muscle. Understanding this helps you realize that your nervous system is a key player in how effectively you train.

When you engage in corrective exercises, you’re not just working on your muscles; you’re also strengthening these connections, enhancing your body's ability to move efficiently and reducing the risk of injury. Over time, this can lead to lasting improvements in your overall movement quality.

**Do this week:** Focus on one corrective exercise that targets a specific area of weakness, and really concentrate on the quality of your movements to enhance that neural connection.
