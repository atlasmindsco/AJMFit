# Drip 035 — Acute pain
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 160. Theme: Assessment & Safety. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Recognize acute pain in training

---

Pain can be a clear signal from your body. **Acute pain** is the normal, short-term pain that indicates a more serious injury or issue. In your training, understanding this type of pain helps you differentiate between typical discomfort from exercise and signs that something is wrong.

If you experience acute pain during a workout, it’s crucial to listen and assess the situation rather than powering through. Ignoring these signals can lead to more serious injuries down the line.

**Do this week:** Pay attention to any unexpected pain during your workouts. If you feel acute pain, stop the activity and evaluate how you feel before continuing.
