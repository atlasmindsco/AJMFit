# Drip 044 — Athletic trainer
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 14. Theme: Training Foundations. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Who helps fix your injuries?

---

When you injure yourself, you want a pro by your side. **Athletic trainers** are health-care professionals trained to prevent and treat physical injuries. They work closely with athletes and active individuals to ensure safe recovery and optimal performance.

For anyone training, understanding the role of athletic trainers is key. If you have an injury, consulting an athletic trainer can provide you with a tailored recovery plan that allows you to return to your workouts safely. This is vital to avoid further injury and ensure you’re training effectively.

**Do this week:** If you're dealing with pain or an injury, seek out a certified athletic trainer for guidance instead of pushing through it on your own.
