# Drip 017 — Calcium storehouse
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 19. Theme: Nutrition & Fuel. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Your bones are more than structure

---

Think of your bones as a living storehouse. **Calcium storehouse** refers to the role of bones in storing calcium and other essential minerals. This storage is vital because it allows the body to access these nutrients when needed, supporting muscle function and nervous system health.

For your training, this means that strong bones are crucial for overall movement and performance. When your calcium levels are low, your bones can weaken, which may impact your strength and stability during workouts.

**Do this week:** Focus on including calcium-rich foods in your diet, like dairy products, leafy greens, or fortified alternatives.
