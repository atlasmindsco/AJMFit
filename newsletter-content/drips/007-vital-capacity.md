# Drip 007 — Vital capacity
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Training Foundations. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Breathe Better, Train Better

---

Breathing is more than just a reflex; it’s a foundation for your workouts. **Vital capacity** is the maximum amount of air you can exhale after taking a full breath in. This measure reflects how well your lungs function, which is critical for delivering oxygen to your muscles during exercise.

When your vital capacity is high, your body can perform more efficiently, allowing you to sustain intensity and recover faster. Poor breathing mechanics can limit your performance and even lead to fatigue during workouts.

**Do this week:** Focus on deep diaphragmatic breathing during your warm-ups. Inhale deeply through your nose, expanding your belly, and exhale fully through your mouth.
