# Drip 008 — Posture
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Mobility & Correctives. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Fix Your Posture, Fix Your Life

---

Your posture shapes not only how you look but also how your body moves and feels. **Posture** refers to the position of your body while standing, sitting, or moving. Poor posture, like forward head posture from smartphone use, can lead to pain and dysfunction in the neck, spine, and shoulders.

For your training, this means being aware of your body’s alignment during workouts and daily activities. Correcting your posture can enhance your performance and reduce your risk of injury, ultimately improving your quality of life.

**Do this week:** Spend five minutes each day practicing standing tall with your shoulders back and head aligned over your spine.
