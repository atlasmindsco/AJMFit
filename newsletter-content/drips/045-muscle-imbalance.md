# Drip 045 — Muscle imbalance
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 9. Theme: Mobility & Correctives. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Fixing Muscle Imbalances for Better Training

---

Picture this: you’re pushing hard in the gym, but something just feels off. This may be due to **muscle imbalance**, which occurs when one muscle is stronger or stiffer than its opposing muscle. This imbalance can lead to poor movement patterns and increase your risk of injury.

For your training, recognizing muscle imbalances is key. They can hinder your progress and affect your overall performance. By addressing these imbalances, you can improve your movement quality and prevent injuries, allowing for more effective workouts.

**Do this week:** Spend a few minutes assessing your body. Identify any areas where you feel tightness or weakness, and incorporate stretches and strengthening exercises for those opposing muscles.
