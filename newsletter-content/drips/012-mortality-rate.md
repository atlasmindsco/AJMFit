# Drip 012 — Mortality rate
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Training Foundations. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How posture impacts your life

---

Poor posture isn't just uncomfortable; it can lead to serious health risks over time, including an increased **mortality rate**. This term refers to the number of deaths within a specific population, and research shows that physical inactivity and poor posture contribute to various health issues that can shorten life expectancy. For your training, this means that addressing your posture and incorporating corrective exercises can improve your overall health and longevity.

**Do this week:** Focus on your posture during daily activities; check in with yourself every hour to ensure you're standing or sitting up straight. 

Next week, we'll explore how to create a balanced exercise routine that supports your posture.
