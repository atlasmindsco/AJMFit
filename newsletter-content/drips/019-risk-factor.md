# Drip 019 — Risk factor
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Assessment & Safety. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Identify and Manage Your Risk Factors

---

Every choice you make in your training can affect your overall health and performance. **Risk factor** refers to any physical, psychological, or environmental element that increases the likelihood of injury or disease. Understanding your risk factors helps you create a safer and more effective training plan.

For example, poor posture can lead to excess stress on your neck and spine, increasing your risk of injury. Being aware of these factors allows you to adjust your exercises and habits to minimize potential harm and enhance your results.

**Do this week:** Assess your daily posture while sitting or standing, and make adjustments to correct any forward head or slouched positions you notice. 

Next week, we’ll dive into how small changes can lead to big improvements in your training.
