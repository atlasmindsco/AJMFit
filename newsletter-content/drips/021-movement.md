# Drip 021 — Movement
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Mobility & Correctives. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Move Better, Train Better

---

Movement is a physical motion occurring at one or more joints, influenced by mobility, stability, posture, and motor control. This means that how well you move directly affects your training performance and injury risk. Improving your movement helps you lift more effectively and recover faster, making each workout more productive.

**Do this week:** Focus on a mobility exercise, like hip circles or shoulder dislocates, to enhance your range of motion before your workouts.

Next week, we'll explore how posture can impact your lifts.
