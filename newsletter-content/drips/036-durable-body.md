# Drip 036 — Durable body
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 12. Theme: Training Foundations. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Build a body that lasts

---

Life can throw unexpected challenges your way, from icy sidewalks to crowded spaces. A **durable body** is one that can handle these unpredictable moments, reducing your risk of injury. This means not only building strength but also ensuring your joints and muscles have the mobility they need to respond effectively. When your nervous system can control muscle activation well, you're better equipped to avoid setbacks.

**Do this week:** Focus on a mix of strength and mobility exercises, like squats combined with hip stretches, to enhance your body’s durability.

Next week, we'll explore how to balance strength training and flexibility for optimal performance.
