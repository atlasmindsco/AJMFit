# Drip 026 — Medications
Source: High Res_CPT, chunk 285. Theme: Cardio & Energy. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How medications impact your heart rate

---

Your heart rate can change based on various factors, including medications. **Medications** can either increase or decrease your heart rate, which can affect how hard your heart works during exercise and throughout the day.

For anyone training, understanding how your medications influence your heart rate is vital. An elevated heart rate can mean you're working harder than intended, while a lower rate may indicate less intensity or even fatigue. This knowledge helps you adjust your workouts to match your body’s needs.

**Do this week:** Check any medications you are taking and research how they might affect your heart rate during exercise.
