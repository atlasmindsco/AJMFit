# Drip 040 — Controls arousal
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 67. Theme: Recovery & Adaptation. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Keep your energy balanced

---

Your body is like a finely tuned machine, and **controls arousal** is a key function that helps manage your energy levels and focus. This means your nervous system regulates how awake or relaxed you feel, influencing your workouts and recovery. If you're too wired, you might struggle to focus; if you're too relaxed, you might not have the energy for that last set. 
**Do this week:** Take five minutes before workouts to practice deep breathing, helping you find a balanced state of arousal for better performance.
