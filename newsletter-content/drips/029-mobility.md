# Drip 029 — Mobility
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Mobility & Correctives. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Unlock Your Movement Potential

---

Imagine trying to lift heavy weights with stiff joints. **Mobility** is the ability to move freely through a normal range of motion using minimal effort. It affects how well your body can perform exercises and daily activities, impacting your strength and overall performance.

For your training, better mobility means you can achieve proper form, reduce the risk of injury, and enhance your workouts. If you're struggling to reach full depth in squats or feeling tight in your shoulders during presses, improving your mobility is key.

**Do this week:** Spend 10 minutes daily on dynamic stretches focusing on your tight areas, like hips or shoulders.
