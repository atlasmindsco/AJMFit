# Drip 010 — High-intensity exercise
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Programming & Variables. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Unlocking Your Power with Intensity

---

High-intensity exercise is a form of exercise that requires a large percentage of a person’s physical power. This means you're pushing your body hard, engaging multiple muscle groups, and maximizing your effort in a shorter time frame. For your training, this can lead to greater strength gains, improved cardiovascular fitness, and enhanced fat loss when done correctly.

**Do this week:** Incorporate one high-intensity session into your routine, focusing on compound movements like squats or deadlifts for 20-30 minutes.

Next week, we'll dive into how to balance intensity with recovery.
