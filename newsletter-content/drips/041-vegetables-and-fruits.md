# Drip 041 — Vegetables and fruits
Source: DGA26-30, chunk 9. Theme: Nutrition & Fuel. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Fuel Your Workouts with Produce

---

Eating a wide variety of **vegetables and fruits** can significantly boost your overall nutrition. These foods are nutrient-dense, which means they provide vitamins, minerals, and fiber without a lot of calories. Incorporating more of these into your meals can help fuel your workouts and support recovery, making it easier to reach your fitness goals.

**Do this week:** Aim to fill half your plate with colorful vegetables and fruits at each meal. Try one new fruit or vegetable you haven't had before.
