# Drip 030 — Motor control
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Anatomy & Movement. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Master Your Muscle Coordination

---

Every movement you make depends on how well your body activates and coordinates its muscles. **Motor control** is the process through which your muscles work together during movement. This coordination is key to performing exercises effectively and safely, impacting everything from lifting weights to everyday activities.

For your training, good motor control means better form, reducing the risk of injury and improving performance. When you can control your movements, you're more likely to lift heavier and achieve your goals.

**Do this week:** Focus on slow, controlled movements during your workouts. This helps reinforce motor control and ensures you are using the right muscles.
