# Drip 009 — Rotator cuff
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 4. Theme: Anatomy & Movement. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Why your shoulders need attention

---

Your shoulders are more than just joints—they're a complex system that includes the **rotator cuff**, a group of tendons and four muscles that connect your upper arm to your shoulder blade. If these muscles aren't functioning well, your shoulder stability and movement can suffer, leading to pain and dysfunction.

For those who train regularly, strong and balanced rotator cuff muscles are crucial for performing lifts and exercises safely. Poor shoulder mechanics can limit your range of motion and increase your risk of injury, especially during overhead movements.

**Do this week:** Focus on exercises that strengthen your rotator cuff, like external rotations with a resistance band, to improve your shoulder health and performance.
