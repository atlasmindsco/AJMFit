# Drip 034 — Stimulants
Source: High Res_CPT, chunk 285. Theme: Cardio & Energy. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How stimulants affect your heart

---

Your heart can race for many reasons, and **stimulants** are one of them. Caffeine, nicotine, and tobacco can all increase your heart rate, which means your body is working harder. For your training, this can impact your cardio performance and recovery, making it crucial to understand how these substances affect you personally.

**Do this week:** Track your heart rate before and after your workouts and note any stimulants you consumed beforehand.
