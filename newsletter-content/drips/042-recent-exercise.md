Source: High Res_CPT, chunk 285. Theme: Cardio & Energy. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How recent exercise impacts heart rate

---

Your heart doesn’t just stop when your workout does. **Recent exercise** refers to the increased heart rate immediately following physical activity. This elevation can last for a while, depending on the intensity of your workout and your fitness level.

For your training, this means you might see higher heart rates during cooldowns or when resting after intense sessions. Understanding how your heart rate responds can help you gauge workout intensity and recovery needs.

**Do this week:** Monitor your heart rate right after your workouts and note how long it takes to return to your resting rate.
