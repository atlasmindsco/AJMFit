# Drip 038 — Motor program
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 14. Theme: Anatomy & Movement. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Unlock automatic movement patterns

---

Imagine effortlessly performing a movement without thinking about each step. This is what a **motor program** is—a relatively automatic movement pattern produced by your nervous system. 

For your training, understanding motor programs means recognizing that your body can learn to execute movements more efficiently with practice. When you repeat a movement, your brain creates a pathway that makes it easier and smoother over time. This can enhance your performance and reduce the risk of injury.

**Do this week:** Choose one exercise you want to improve, like squats or push-ups, and practice it consistently, focusing on form and rhythm—aim for at least three sessions. 

Next week, we'll explore how to break bad movement habits.
