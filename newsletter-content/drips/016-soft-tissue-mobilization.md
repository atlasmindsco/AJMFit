# Drip 016 — Soft tissue mobilization
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 14. Theme: Recovery & Adaptation. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Relieve tension with this technique

---

After a tough workout, your muscles might feel tight and restricted, limiting your recovery. **Soft tissue mobilization** is the process of removing restrictions in muscles and connective tissues to help restore normal function. It’s a technique typically used by healthcare professionals like chiropractors and physical therapists, not personal trainers, who should focus on verbal coaching and minimal physical feedback.

For your training, think about how addressing tightness can enhance your performance. A proper recovery routine will help you move better and reduce the risk of injury.

**Do this week:** Spend a few minutes after your workouts using a foam roller or massage ball to target any tight areas in your muscles. 

Keep prioritizing recovery, and next week we’ll explore another way to boost your training results.
