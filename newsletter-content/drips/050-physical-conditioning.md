# Drip 050 — Physical conditioning
Source: High Res_CPT, chunk 481. Theme: Cardio & Energy. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Boost your lung capacity now

---

Breathing deeply during exercise is more than just filling your lungs; it’s about how your body adapts to physical conditioning. **Physical conditioning** refers to the improvement of your body's capacity to perform aerobic exercise, which can enhance lung capacity by up to 15 percent. When you engage in regular aerobic activities, your lungs become more efficient, allowing you to take in more oxygen and expel carbon dioxide effectively. 

This means that as you train, your body gets better at utilizing oxygen, which can improve your endurance and overall performance. 

**Do this week:** Incorporate 20-30 minutes of aerobic exercise into your routine, focusing on maintaining a steady pace that challenges your breathing.
