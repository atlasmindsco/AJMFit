# Drip 028 — Olympic lifts
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Training Foundations. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Unlock your power with Olympic lifts

---

Imagine harnessing your full physical potential with every lift. **Olympic lifts** are the snatch and the clean and jerk, exercises that demand significant power and technique. Incorporating these lifts into your routine not only builds strength but also improves your mobility and coordination, making you a more efficient athlete.

**Do this week:** Try adding one Olympic lift to your training session, focusing on form and technique rather than weight.
