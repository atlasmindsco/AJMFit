# Drip 011 — Physiatrist
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Recovery & Adaptation. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Restore Your Function After Injury

---

When injuries occur, your body needs a path back to normal function. A **physiatrist** is a physician who specializes in restoring that function to the bones, muscles, and nervous system. They focus on rehabilitation through exercise and other non-surgical methods, allowing you to regain strength and mobility after an injury.

For your training, understanding the role of a physiatrist can help you appreciate the importance of recovery and adaptation. If you're dealing with pain or limited movement, seeking out a physiatrist can provide you with a tailored approach to your rehabilitation, ensuring that you return to your workouts safely and effectively.

**Do this week:** If you're feeling any discomfort, take the time to assess your movements and consider consulting with a professional who can help guide your recovery.
