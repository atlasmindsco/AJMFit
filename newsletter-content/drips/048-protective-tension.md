# Drip 048 — Protective tension
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 333. Theme: Recovery & Adaptation. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How stiffness can help you recover

---

Imagine your body as a finely tuned instrument, where stiffness plays a key role in maintaining control. **Protective tension** refers to the stiffness within your soft tissues that limits your mobility. This tension can actually help stabilize your joints and protect against injury, especially during workouts.

For your training, understanding protective tension means recognizing that some stiffness can be beneficial. It helps activate muscles that support your joints, which can improve your overall performance and reduce pain during movement. Rather than forcing mobility, focus on controlled movements that engage these stabilizing muscles.

**Do this week:** Incorporate gentle stretching and mobility exercises that emphasize control and stability rather than pushing for full range of motion. 

Next week, we’ll explore how to balance mobility and stability for optimal performance.
