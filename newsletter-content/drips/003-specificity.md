# Drip 003 — Specificity
Source: ISSA CPT, Ch.9 — Principle of Specificity. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Train for the thing you actually want

---

The ISSA **principle of specificity** is short and unforgiving: the adaptations you get are based on the type of training you do.

Train for endurance, you get endurance. Train for max strength, you get strength. There's no accidental six-pack from a marathon plan, and no surprise marathon time from heavy triples. Your body adapts to exactly the demand you place on it — no more, no less.

Most people train *generally* and hope *specifically*. They do a bit of everything, then wonder why nothing moves. The fix isn't more effort. It's aim.

The cert also says to start where you actually are: begin with training that fits your current level, then progress to harder work as fitness develops. Specific *and* honest about today.

**Do this week:** Write down your single main goal in one sentence — "get stronger," "build my chest," "run a 5K without stopping." Then look at last week's training and ask: did it actually look like that goal? Cut one thing that didn't belong.
