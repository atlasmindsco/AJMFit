# Drip 032 — Law
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 25. Theme: Recovery & Adaptation. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How bones adapt to stress

---

Bones are living tissues that respond to the demands placed on them. **Wolff’s Law** states that bone will adapt to the loads it experiences, thickening under stress or thinning when inactive. This means that if you train consistently and apply the right loads, your bones will strengthen. Conversely, if you are sedentary, your bones can weaken over time due to resorption.

**Do this week:** Include weight-bearing exercises in your routine, such as squats or lunges, to help stimulate bone deposition.
