# Drip 031 — Creep
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 34. Theme: Programming & Variables. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How tissue adapts to pressure

---

When you hold a stretch, your tissues respond in a fascinating way. **Creep** is the temporary deformation of connective tissue under sustained load. This means that when you apply a consistent stretch to a muscle or ligament, it can gradually lengthen over time without permanent damage.

For your training, this underscores the importance of holding stretches longer to improve flexibility and mobility. Instead of rushing through your stretching routine, give your muscles the time they need to adapt and elongate.

**Do this week:** Spend an extra 30 seconds holding each stretch during your cool down.
