# Drip 022 — Strength
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Anatomy & Movement. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Strength: Your Key to Power

---

Strength is the maximal force that a muscle or muscle group can generate. This means when you train for strength, you're working to lift heavier loads and push your body to its limits. Building strength not only improves your performance in exercises like squats and deadlifts but also supports your daily activities and overall health.

**Do this week:** Focus on a compound lift, such as the squat or bench press, and aim to increase your weight by 5% from last week. 

Next week, we'll explore how mobility plays a role in your strength training.
