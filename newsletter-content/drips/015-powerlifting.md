# Drip 015 — Powerlifting
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Programming & Variables. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Unlock your strength with powerlifting

---

Imagine lifting the heaviest weight you can for just one rep. **Powerlifting** is a strength sport focused on squats, deadlifts, and bench presses, where the goal is to lift the most weight possible for a single repetition. This means training your body to generate maximal force, improving not just strength but also your technique and body mechanics during these movements.

For your training, powerlifting can help you focus on key lifts that build overall strength and muscle mass. It emphasizes proper form and control, ensuring you maximize your potential while minimizing injury risk.

**Do this week:** Choose one of the powerlifting lifts and practice it for 3 sets of 5 reps, focusing on perfecting your form.
