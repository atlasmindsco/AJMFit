# Drip 004 — Reversibility
Source: ISSA CPT, Ch.9 — Principle of Reversibility & detraining. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Use it or lose it (literally)

---

The ISSA **principle of reversibility** is the one nobody wants to hear: you lose the effects of training after you stop. The cert calls the slide **detraining**, and it sets in after about **two weeks** off.

This is why the person who trains at 70% every single week quietly buries the one who goes 100% for ten days and then vanishes. Adaptation is a tenant, not an owner. Stop paying rent and it moves out.

It's also good news. It means the bar to *hold* your progress is low — far lower than the bar to build it. A couple of short, honest sessions a week is enough to keep what you've earned, even in a busy stretch.

**Do this week:** Look at your next 7 days and protect two non-negotiable sessions — even 25 minutes counts. The goal isn't a perfect week. It's not handing back what you've already built.

To whom much is given, much is required — including showing up when it's inconvenient.
