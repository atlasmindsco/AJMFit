# Drip 018 — Cardiovascular endurance
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 201. Theme: Cardio & Energy. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Boost Your Heart's Performance

---

Imagine your heart, lungs, and blood vessels working together like a finely tuned engine. **Cardiovascular endurance** is the ability of these systems to deliver oxygen to your body’s tissues. This endurance is key for supporting your workouts and everyday activities. Better cardiovascular endurance means improved energy levels, faster recovery, and enhanced performance in both cardio and strength training.

**Do this week:** Include at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity cardio, such as brisk walking or cycling, into your weekly routine.
