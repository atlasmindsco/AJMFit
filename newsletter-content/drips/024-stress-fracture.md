# Drip 024 — Stress fracture
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 25. Theme: Recovery & Adaptation. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** How to avoid stress fractures

---

Imagine your bones as living structures that respond to the pressure and stress you put on them. A **stress fracture** is a tiny crack in a bone that develops from repeated strain, often due to overuse or insufficient recovery. This occurs when the accumulation of microdamage surpasses the body’s ability to repair itself.

For your training, this means you must pay attention to how hard and how often you train. If you push too hard without enough recovery, you risk these small but painful fractures. Balancing intensity and recovery is key to keeping your bones healthy.

**Do this week:** Incorporate two rest days into your training routine to allow your bones time to recover and adapt. 

Keep an eye on your body's signals, and next week we'll explore how to strengthen your bones effectively.
