# Drip 051 — Vastus lateralis OVERHEAD SQUAT ASSESSMENT
Source: High Res_CPT, chunk 317. Theme: Assessment & Safety. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Spot issues in your squat

---

You might think you're just squatting, but the **overhead squat assessment** reveals much more. This assessment observes how well you can maintain proper form while squatting with your arms overhead. If your arms fall forward or you lean too much, it signals potential weaknesses or tightness in your core and shoulder muscles.

For your training, this means understanding your body's limitations and addressing any imbalances. Proper form is key to preventing injuries and ensuring your workouts are effective.

**Do this week:** Try performing an overhead squat in front of a mirror. Pay attention to your arm position and overall form as you squat down. Notice any shifts in your posture.

Next week, we'll look at how to correct those issues.
