# Drip 020 — Intervertebral disc
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Training Foundations. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Why your spine needs support

---

Your spine relies on a unique structure to stay healthy. **Intervertebral discs** are the shock-absorbing, gel-filled structures between each vertebra. They help cushion your spine during movement and protect your nervous system.

Understanding the role of these discs can reshape how you approach your training. Poor posture or excessive strain can lead to disc issues, resulting in pain and decreased mobility. Focusing on proper posture and balanced exercise can help maintain the health of these discs.

**Do this week:** Incorporate exercises that strengthen your core and promote good posture, like planks or bridges, to support your intervertebral discs.
