# Drip 025 — Elastin
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 33. Theme: Nutrition & Fuel. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Why you need elastin for movement

---

Think of elastin as the rubber band in your connective tissues. **Elastin** is an elastic protein that gives tissues like ligaments the ability to stretch and return to their original shape. This means your joints can move freely without losing stability, which is vital for maintaining good form during workouts and daily activities.

When you train, healthy levels of elastin help your ligaments adapt to stress, improving overall joint function and reducing injury risk. Focus on nutrition that supports elastin production, like vitamin C and protein, to keep your connective tissues functioning well.

**Do this week:** Incorporate foods rich in vitamin C, such as oranges or bell peppers, into your meals to support elastin production.
