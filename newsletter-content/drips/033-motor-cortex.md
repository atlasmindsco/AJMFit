# Drip 033 — Motor cortex
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 84. Theme: Nutrition & Fuel. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Control your movement with the brain

---

Your brain is wired to control movement, and a key player in this process is the **motor cortex**. This area of the brain is responsible for planning, controlling, and executing voluntary movements. It helps determine which muscles to activate and when to activate them, ensuring smooth and coordinated motion. 

For your training, understanding the motor cortex means recognizing that effective movement starts in the brain. When you focus on your technique and consciously engage your muscles, you enhance the signals from your motor cortex, leading to better performance and muscle recruitment. 

**Do this week:** Spend a few minutes in each workout to consciously think about the muscles you are using during each exercise. Focus on how they feel and how they move.
