# Drip 027 — Medical doctor
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 6. Theme: Assessment & Safety. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Why your doctor matters in training

---

Every time you lift weights or push your body, you are at risk for injury. That’s where a **medical doctor** comes in. This type of physician specializes in treating diseases and injuries, ensuring you stay healthy and safe while training.

Understanding the role of a medical doctor is key to your training. They can help you address any pre-existing conditions, injuries, or concerns that might affect your performance or safety. Regular check-ups can also ensure you're in the best shape to tackle your fitness goals.

**Do this week:** Schedule a visit with your doctor to discuss any health concerns that may impact your training.
