# Drip 037 — Movement dysfunction
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 9. Theme: Mobility & Correctives. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Fix Your Movement Flaws

---

Imagine struggling to perform a simple squat because your body won't cooperate. **Movement dysfunction** is when you can't execute an exercise properly due to issues with mobility, stability, posture, or motor control. This can lead to ineffective workouts and even injuries.

For your training, it means focusing on correcting these flaws before pushing harder. If you ignore these dysfunctions, your progress will stall and you may develop pain or injury over time.

**Do this week:** Spend 10 minutes assessing your squat form in front of a mirror. Look for any misalignments in your knees, back, and feet. 

Next week, we'll dive into how to improve your mobility.
