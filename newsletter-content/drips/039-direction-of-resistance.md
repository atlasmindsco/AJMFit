# Drip 039 — Direction of resistance
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 105. Theme: Programming & Variables. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Master your resistance direction

---

Every time you lift weights or use bands, you're dealing with the **direction of resistance**. This concept refers to the direction and magnitude of the load created by these tools. Understanding this helps you tailor your exercises for better alignment and effectiveness in your workouts.

When you know how the resistance is directed, you can choose exercises that align with your movement goals, whether it’s building strength, enhancing stability, or improving athletic performance. This awareness can also help prevent injury by ensuring you're not forcing your body into awkward positions against the load.

**Do this week:** Experiment with a resistance band or free weights and notice how changing the angle of your movements alters the resistance you feel.

Next week, we’ll dive into how to apply these concepts for better performance.
