# Drip 013 — Forward head posture
Source: Corrective Exercise Full Textbook, chunk 5. Theme: Mobility & Correctives. Send: Thursday.

**Subject:** Fix your forward head posture now

---

Imagine staring down at your phone for hours; that's how **forward head posture** develops. This condition occurs when your cervical spine shifts forward, putting strain on your neck, spine, and shoulders. It's become common due to our daily habits, and it can lead to discomfort and health issues if left unaddressed.

For your training, correcting forward head posture can enhance your overall mobility and reduce the risk of injury. Improving this position can also help you breathe more effectively and lift more efficiently in your workouts.

**Do this week:** Spend a few minutes each day stretching your neck and chest while consciously pulling your shoulders back and down.
