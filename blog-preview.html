AJM Fit Is Live

The doors are open. Your training, your nutrition, and a direct line to your coach — finally in one place.

AM
Coach Anthony Martin
Founder, AJM Fit

After months of building, AJM Fit is officially live. Not another app full of generic workouts you'll ignore by week two — a coaching system built around you. Your goals, your schedule, your starting point.

What's inside

A program built for you. No copy-paste templates. I look at where you are and where you want to be, and I build the plan to bridge it — then adjust it as you go.

Nutrition that fits your life. Log meals, track your macros, and hit your targets without weighing every gram or obsessing over a number. Scan a barcode, search a food, done.

A direct line to me. Message me right inside the app. Questions, check-ins, accountability — you're not doing this alone, and you're not waiting days for a reply.

A community that shows up. Wins, events, and real people training alongside you. Motivation hits different when you're not the only one in the room.

Chea, your AI assistant. Quick answers on form, nutrition, or where to find something in the app — any time, day or night.

How to start

Apply in about two minutes. I read every application personally — if we're the right fit, you're in, and we'll get your plan built and your first call on the calendar.

This is the part most people overthink. Don't. The hardest rep is the decision to begin — everything after that, we handle together.
